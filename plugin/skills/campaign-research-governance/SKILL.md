# Campaign research governance

Use this skill for literature, data provenance, method choice, source resolution, claims, synthesis, and publication-facing work.

Call `campaign_preflight` before research work. For `research` and `research_delivery`, the campaign requires Co-Researcher method work. Follow the applicable Co-Researcher skill instead of inventing a local substitute.

Record receipts for:

- source identity and retrieval location;
- method choice and assumptions;
- claim-to-source support;
- uncertainty, exclusions, and unresolved contradictions;
- citation or academic-integrity review.

Search results, memory, agent summaries, and a database row are leads until the underlying evidence is resolved. Do not present an unsupported inference as a source fact. If a method or source gate is not passed, keep the campaign at the current gate and report the single next permitted action.
